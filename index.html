Quick answers

Why did my Medicare premium go up?

CMS sets the standard Part B premium every year. CMS says the 2027 increase is mainly due to projected increases in healthcare spending.

Does my Social Security check go up in 2027?

Most people get the 3.6% cost-of-living adjustment. If your Part B premium comes out of your check, the premium increase is subtracted from that raise. Whether your net check goes up depends on your benefit amount.

Do I pay the same premium as everyone else?

Not always. Higher-income people pay an extra amount called IRMAA, based on income from two years earlier. Most people pay the standard amount.

Where do these numbers come from?

From Social Security's 2027 fact sheet and CMS announcements, as reported by AARP and other sources. Every figure is listed on the Sources page, with a note on whether it has been confirmed on the official site yet.
