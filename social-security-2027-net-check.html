How this estimate works

1. The raise

Your current benefit times 3.6%, the 2027 cost-of-living adjustment.

2. The premium

The 2027 standard Part B premium of $209.40, compared with $202.90 in 2026. It's subtracted only if the premium comes out of your check.

3. Your net check

The raised benefit minus the 2027 premium, compared with your current benefit minus the 2026 premium.

Estimates assume the standard premium. If you pay the income-related surcharge (IRMAA), your premium is higher. Hold-harmless protection is not modeled. Social Security rounds benefit amounts down to the next whole dollar, so your deposit may differ slightly. This is an estimate, not a benefit statement.

Common questions

Where do I find my current benefit?

On your Social Security statement at ssa.gov, or in your my Social Security account. Use the amount you receive before the 2027 raise.

What if I have a Medicare Advantage plan?

Many Medicare Advantage plans still have Part B deducted from your check. This calculator covers only the Part B premium. It doesn't include any separate plan premium.

What if my check goes down?

Hold-harmless rules may limit the Part B increase so your check doesn't shrink. Ask SSA if you're not sure whether it applies to you.

Is my information stored?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved.
